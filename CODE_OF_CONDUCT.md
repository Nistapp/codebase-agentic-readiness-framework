# Code of Conduct

This project is a place to improve codebases for AI coding agents. Everyone who takes part follows this code: in
issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space. It binds people and the AI agents they run.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Criticise the work, never the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give evidence for a claim, and accept evidence against your own.
- Welcome newcomers. Explain a term or a rule when someone does not know it.
- Keep a discussion on its topic.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, or threats, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Pressure on someone to merge, approve or drop a change for reasons other than its merits.

## How to report a problem

Report it privately, not in a public issue. Email <udan-security@nistapp.com> with "Conduct" in the subject. The
maintainer reads every report, keeps the reporter's name private, and replies within 5 working days.

## What the maintainer may do

The maintainer may edit or remove a comment, close an issue or a pull request, or block a person from the
project's spaces. The maintainer acts in proportion to the problem and tells the person why. Who the maintainer is
and how the project decides is in [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md).
